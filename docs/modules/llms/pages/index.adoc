An LLM is a next-word predictor trained on a vast amount of text, then taught to be an assistant. This part opens the box, layer by layer.
--

NOTE: The lessons in this part are being written. The outline below is the plan; until each lesson lands, the links under *Go deeper* cover the same ground.

== Lessons

. Embeddings: meaning as a position in space
. Attention and the Transformer, by intuition
. Pretraining: predicting the next token at scale
. From base model to assistant: fine-tuning, RLHF and constitutions
. Sampling: temperature, top-p, and why answers vary
. Context windows and their limits
. Hallucination: why fluent is not the same as true
. Reasoning models: thinking before answering
. Open and closed models, sizes, and how to choose
. Lab (Python): a tiny character-level language model from scratch
