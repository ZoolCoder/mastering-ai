= Learning paths
:description: Five routes through the course, depending on who you are and what you want to do with AI.

Every path starts at part 1. The later parts each path skips are still there
when you want them.

== Everyone: use AI well in work and life

For anyone, technical or not, who wants to understand AI and use it with
confidence. About 15 hours.

== Developer: build with AI and code with agents

For developers who want to add AI to their apps and work well with coding
agents. About 40 hours.

Parts 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 6 → 7 → 9.

== AI engineer: build AI products

For developers who want to build AI products for a living: retrieval, agents,
evals. About 60 hours.

Parts 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 9 → 11.

== Harness engineer: make agents reliable

For people who already build with agents and want them to finish long tasks
without supervision. About 30 hours.

Parts 1 (skim) → 3 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 → 11.

== Full mastery

Everything, in order: parts 1 to 11. About 80 hours. At the end you can explain
every layer, from a single neuron to a multi-agent harness, and build each one.

[TIP]
====
Not sure which path to pick? Start with *Developer* if you write code, and
*Everyone* if you do not. You can switch paths at any point; none of them
repeat work.
====
