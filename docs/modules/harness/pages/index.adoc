= Part 8 · Harness engineering
:description: Guides and sensors, long-running harnesses, loops and graphs.
:navtitle: 8 · Harness engineering

[.hero]
--
Agent = model + harness. The harness is everything around the model: instructions, tools, environment, state and feedback. Strong models fail in weak harnesses.
--

NOTE: The lessons in this part are being written. The outline below is the plan; until each lesson lands, the links under *Go deeper* cover the same ground.

== Lessons

. Why capable agents still fail
. What a harness is: the five subsystems
. The repository as the system of record
. Instructions that scale: maps, not manuals
. Continuity across sessions: progress files and handoffs
. Initialisation: give each session a clean start
. Scope control: one feature at a time, and feature lists
. No premature victory: definitions of done
. Feedback: tests, end-to-end checks and observability
. Loop engineering: Ralph loops, goals and maker-checker
. Graph engineering: plans as graphs of agents
. Inside real harnesses: Claude Code, Codex and others
. Lab (TypeScript): build a harness that ships a small app across many sessions

== After this part you can

* Diagnose an agent failure as a harness problem and fix the right subsystem.
* Design a harness for a long-running task: instructions, state, checks and a loop.
* Choose between one loop, maker-checker and a graph of agents.
